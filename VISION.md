# Vision

Our vision is to bring the classic, face-to-face tabletop experience of chess to modern large-format touchscreen devices.

Most digital chess games are designed for a single user staring at a vertical or horizontal screen, playing against an AI or a remote opponent over the internet. This project is specifically designed for **local multiplayer on a single shared touchscreen**, laid flat like a traditional game board.

## Core Principles
1. **Shared Physical Space:** The application assumes players are sharing the physical device, sitting opposite each other. The UI must accommodate this by ensuring neither player has to read text or interact with controls that are upside-down for them.
2. **Tactile and Intuitive:** Moving pieces should feel natural on a touchscreen—supporting dragging, dropping, and tapping.
3. **Frictionless Experience:** No accounts, no servers, no complex lobbies. Open the app, and the board is ready.
4. **Privacy & Reliability:** 100% client-side application hosted statically. The game should work flawlessly entirely offline via PWA capabilities.
